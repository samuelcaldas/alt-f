Donations & Community Credits

Supporting the ongoing development of Alt-F and recognizing community contributions and hardware donations.

Supporting Alt-F Development

"You can buy me a beer, to help support developing Alt-F — hmmm, on second thoughts it is better to not drink beer while developing :-) . Instead of beer, donations will be used for buying new hardware."

If you have hardware that you do not use anymore (such as the DNS-321, DNS-323-rev-C1, DNS-320-rev-Ax, or spare disk drives) and you live in Europe, hardware donations help maintain and expand board support.

Community & Porting Credits

Alt-F builds upon the dedicated work of the open-source embedded Linux community:

M. Michlmayr

Debian ARM kernel and bootloader porting work on the D-Link DNS-323 (cyrius.com).

J. Lentin

Linux kernel device tree integration and driver support for DNS-320 and DNS-325 (jamie.lentin.co.uk).

A. Böhler

Initial Linux kernel porting, U-Boot environment analysis, and hacks on the DNS-320L (aboehler.at).

L. Martignoni

DNS-320L hardware porting and open-source bootloader investigations (github.com/martignlo/DNS-320L).

D. Grande

Hardware modifications, LED management, and kernel enhancements on the DNS-325 (github.com/davidedg/NAS-DNS325-mod).

Necromant

Marvell Armada 370 Debian bringup and device tree implementation for the DNS-327L (ncrmnt.org).

The Community Forum

Ongoing testing, bug reports, and discussion on the DNS-323 Forum and Alt-F Google Group.

The DNS-323 Wiki

Foundational technical documentation on hardware layouts, GPIO pins, and bootloaders (dns323.kood.org).
